The old lighthouse stood at the edge of the rocky coast where the waves had been crashing for centuries. Every morning the keeper climbed the narrow stairs to check the great lamp that guided ships safely through the dark waters. He had done this simple task for over thirty years and he knew every creak of the wooden steps by heart. The wind outside was strong that day and the sea looked restless under the gray sky. Seagulls circled above the tower calling out to each other as if they were sharing some secret news. Down in the small village nearby fishermen were preparing their boats for another long day at sea. They trusted the light to bring them home when fog rolled in unexpectedly. The keeper often thought about all the sailors whose lives depended on his careful attention to the flame. It was a quiet responsibility but one that filled him with a sense of purpose. As the sun began to set he lit the lamp once more and watched its beam sweep across the water.
